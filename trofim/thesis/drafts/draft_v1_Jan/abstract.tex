In this thesis, we investigate how disorder influences the dynamics observed in attosecond experiments. To address this problem, we perform numerical simulations within density functional theory using the SALMON software package. In semiconductors, our analysis focuses on electron wave-packet damping, current relaxation, and charge-carrier photoinjection. This study offers the first systematic assessment of how disorder shapes current dynamics in attosecond-scale measurements.
